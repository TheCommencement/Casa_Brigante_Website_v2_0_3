# Casa Brigantè — Creative Experience Standard v1.0

*The governing standard for every creative property the house presents. Ratified by the founder. Rules exist before rooms.*

---

## The Founding Sentence

**Casa Brigantè presents worlds, not products. Products may be purchased; worlds are experienced.**

This is the reason the book pages read as exhibits instead of storefronts, and it governs Music, Audio Dramas, and every division that follows.

---

## Two Identities, Always Simultaneous

### 1. Institutional Identity — Constant

Never changes across any property: header, navigation, footer, typography, grid, spacing, editorial tone, gold institutional accents, estate watermark, CTA pattern, component behavior.

Visitors must always know they are inside Casa Brigantè.

**The Gold Rule.** Gold is the institutional constant, the equivalent of Penguin orange, Criterion black, Ferrari red. It owns the institutional layer specifically (header, estate mark, dividers, buy and listen actions) and is never reassigned to a property.

### 2. Property Identity — Variable

Each property owns these, and they are never interchangeable:

- One defining visual anchor
- One atmospheric accent color (secondary to gold; owns mood and texture only)
- One motion language
- One environmental texture
- One narrative voice
- One emotional identity

---

## Motion Is Identity

Motion is not decoration. Motion communicates character. Someone should recognize the property even if every page were grayscale.

- **VANTA** — deliberate, restrained transitions; minimal animation; precision and control.
- **MOONREALM** — slow ambient drift; light bloom; lunar atmosphere.
- **Solana Reyes** — almost imperceptible movement; film grain; dust; paper texture; long pauses.

All motion respects reduced-motion preferences.

---

## The Anchor Principle

Every creative property must have one definitive image, chosen with book-cover-level care. It becomes the canonical visual reference for the website hero, press kit, streaming platforms, social media, marketing, and internal documentation. Everything else may evolve. The anchor remains. It is the artist's equivalent of a book cover.

---

## Identity Before Inventory

A page never exists merely because content might arrive later. A property launches only when it possesses a complete narrative identity, a canonical visual anchor, a finished world, and meaningful content. Additional releases enrich the experience; they do not define it. Build fully, one property at a time. Never launch empty rooms.

---

## Solana First

Solana Reyes is the proof of concept. She demonstrates that Casa Brigantè builds interconnected intellectual property, not a media display: a visitor moves from *A Song She Wrote* to Solana Reyes the artist to future recordings and performances without ever leaving one narrative universe. If that experience works, the architecture is validated for every future original property.

---

## Why This Standard Exists

Rules before rooms, always. Constitution before company. Design system before components. Component library before pages. Governance before operations. Brand standards before logos. Every division follows the same order.
