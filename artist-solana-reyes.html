Solana Reyes

The voice prison couldn't silence.

Music became the one place they couldn't lock me out.

Solana Reyes is the voice at the center of the novel A Song She Wrote, serving five life sentences while her music refuses to stay behind the walls. She never wanted the attention. She wanted the songs to speak for themselves.

The Voice

Raised in Mesa, Arizona, on church on Sundays and a grandmother's vinyl. Her uncle taught her to feel bass and rhythm on a Casio keyboard before she ever touched a real studio. She writes better than she performs, produces better than she raps, and has never once chased a viral moment. Guarded, observant, deadly calm, and loyal to blood past the point it was ever returned.

Her people call themselves The Yard.
