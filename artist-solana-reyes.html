Solana Reyes

Her World

A woman whose music escaped long before she did.

Raised in Mesa, Arizona, Solana Reyes learned rhythm through her uncle’s vinyl and a secondhand keyboard, and learned early that being seen clearly could be dangerous. A producer’s ear, a ghostwriter’s pen, and a refusal to beg for attention made her one of the most gifted writers few people ever knew by name.

Today she writes from a cell in Arizona, serving five life sentences. The work keeps leaving the building without her. Every song is a survival tactic. Every word is a step closer to free.
