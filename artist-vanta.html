VANTA

Four souls. One echo.

Who They Are

VANTA was never assembled. It emerged.

Four distinct artists bound by a shared obsession with music that lingers long after the final note. Their work exists between elegance and darkness, intimacy and spectacle, where vulnerability becomes power and silence carries as much weight as sound.

Nothing about VANTA asks for attention. Everything about them earns it.

The World

One room, one honest thing.

Every release begins the same way: four perspectives, one room, and the pursuit of something honest. Working alongside Moon Realm, VANTA builds records that are cinematic, emotional, and unmistakably their own. Every lyric is intentional. Every visual is deliberate. Every performance invites interpretation instead of explanation.

Their music isn't made to fill silence. It's made to leave an echo after it.

The Members

Aria Vale 🤍

The Voice

Lead Vocalist · Songwriter

Aria gives language to the emotions most people struggle to name. As VANTA's principal lyricist, she writes with quiet precision, transforming memory, love, grief, and hope into songs that feel deeply personal. Her voice doesn't overpower. It pulls listeners closer.

Juno Knox 🖤

The Architect

Producer · Creative Architect

Juno shapes the world around every song. From production to arrangement, she obsesses over every texture, every transition, and every detail until the music says exactly what words cannot. If Aria writes the story, Juno builds the universe it lives in.

Nyla Starr ⭐

The Spark

Performance Director · Choreographer

Nyla is movement in its purest form. She translates rhythm into emotion and transforms every performance into something unforgettable. On stage she is fearless, magnetic, and impossible to ignore, bringing the group's music fully to life.

Elara Bloom 🌸

The Dreamer

Vocalist · Visual Director

Elara sees beauty where others overlook it. She shapes the visual language of VANTA, balancing softness with mystery and intimacy with grandeur. Her quiet presence anchors the group, reminding everyone that the strongest voices aren't always the loudest.
