VANTA

Four voices. One frequency.

The Statement

We don’t follow the light. We create our own.

The World

Born in the late nights.

VANTA is four voices, four energies, one frequency. Built in the studio with Moon Realm, they turn emotion into sound and chaos into art. Their music lives in the space between darkness and desire.

Every song is a confession. Every performance is a release. This is more than music. This is VANTA.

The Members

Aria Vale 🤍

The Voice

Lead Vocalist · Lyricist

Aria is the heart of VANTA, the writer and the storyteller. Every lyric she writes carries truth, memory, and feeling. She speaks for the group and carries the weight of every word. When Aria sings, the silence listens.

Juno Knox 🖤

The Architect

Producer · Engineer

Juno is the architect behind VANTA’s sound. Precision is her language and control is her art. She builds worlds others can feel. Nothing escapes her ear, and nothing meets her standard until it is right.

Nyla Starr ⭐

The Spark

Performer · Dancer

Nyla turns music into movement. She owns every stage she steps on, and her energy carries the room with her. She lives in the moment and makes everyone in it feel the same. She is the heartbeat of VANTA.

Elara Bloom 🌸

The Dreamer

Vocalist · Visual Director

Elara sees the world differently. She turns feeling into art and art into something timeless. The youngest of VANTA, she carries a quiet wisdom beyond her years. Her voice is softness, and her presence is the calm in the storm.
