A Song She Wrote

Aries Brigantè

Some songs become hits. Others become history.

PaperbackAmazon Kindle EditionAmazon Kindle

Solana never wanted attention. She wanted the music to speak for itself. A brilliant producer and songwriter hiding in plain sight, she’s spent years perfecting her craft while the world rewards louder voices with less talent.

Ahmad has the hunger, the lyrics, and the dream, but talent alone has never been enough.

When a chance encounter at an underground open mic brings them together, they begin chasing something bigger than fame. As success, love, ambition, and personal demons collide, they’ll discover that the songs people remember most often come from the pain they never wanted to survive.

A Song She Wrote is a raw, emotionally charged novel about music, purpose, mental health, and what it truly costs to leave your mark on the world.

Chapter One · Pizza Time

Hot oven air. Hennessy breath. Weed smoke fighting ambition. It was Tuesday night at Pizza Time, a mom-and-pop slice shop by day, underground open mic makeshift club by night. They served $3 slices, poured heavy at the bar, and let anybody with bars touch the stage. You could buy a large pepperoni and lose your soul in the same motherfuckin corner.

The floor was sticky. The mic was dented. The bass rattled like it hated the walls, and the walls knew that shit. This place wasn’t terrible for the Arizona underground rap scene. And Solana came every week just to feel... something.

She sat at the bar alone, hoodie on, headphones around her neck, a glass of Henny sweating beside her. Thick in all the right places, ass like a comma, thighs poured into her black leggings. No flirt in her. Just presence. Hair pulled up, pretty coils wild and pinned like they might fight back.
