From the opening pages

Chapter One · Valencia Heat

The AC rattled like that bitch had asthma.

Devin woke up sweating anyway, like the room had been leaning over him all night, pressing its dirty breath into his pores. The motel squatted off Valencia Road, three stories of peeled paint and warped railings, concrete baked so long it looked permanently pissed off.

The ice machine had been coughing since midnight, metal grinding on metal, spitting cubes for people who needed their cheap liquor cold so they could pretend their life was not a flaming mess. Through the wall to his left, banda music throbbed steady and shameless, brass and accordion bleeding through drywall thin as paper. On the other side, life unfolded without shame too, loud and sloppy and too honest for that hour. Laughter too bright to be real. A headboard knocking rhythm into plaster. A woman’s voice in Spanish, low at first, then climbing, like if she pushed hard enough her words might travel all the way back over the border and fix whatever she left behind.
