Loyal 2 the Lie

Aries Brigantè

Raised in the streets of West Palm Beach, Tariq Harris Jr. has spent his entire life chasing an opportunity few people ever get. As his rise from overlooked prospect to football superstar captures the nation’s attention, fame, fortune, and limitless opportunity finally seem within reach.

But success attracts more than admiration.

Behind the cameras, endorsements, and headlines lies a world where ambition is exploited, loyalty is tested, and trust becomes the most dangerous gamble of all. With every victory pulling him further from the life he once knew, Tariq is forced to confront a painful question: when the people closest to you become your greatest threat, who do you trust?

Loyal 2 the Lie is a gripping novel about family, fame, betrayal, manipulation, and the devastating consequences of staying loyal to the wrong lie.

Chapter One · Ugly Florida Boy

Tariq Harris Jr. was raised where ambition got you shot and talent made you a target.

West Palm Beach wasn’t palm trees and postcards. It was cracked pavement and plastic chairs on porches, where every nigga over 18 either sold weed, rocked a lanyard and had a city job, or talked about what he “coulda been” over loud domino games and blunts.

His pops, Tariq Harris Sr., used to burn rubber on the track like it owed him money. Back in his day, he was that dude, state champion sprinter, full ride to Florida A&M before a pulled hamstring derailed everything. He swore he could’ve gone Olympic if life hadn’t caught him slipping.

Now? Thirty-five, washed up, still living in West Palm. Smoked Black & Milds, weed, and whatever the fuck else, and dropped half-wisdoms that sounded more like warnings than advice.
