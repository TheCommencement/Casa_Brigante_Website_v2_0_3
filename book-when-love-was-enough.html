When Love Was Enough

Aries Brigantè

Being loved is not the same as being valued.

PaperbackAmazon Kindle EditionAmazon Kindle
Published byExcelsa Cornua Rights Group
StatusAvailable now

Kimberly has spent so much of her life surviving that she no longer remembers what it feels like to simply live. Between the weight of single motherhood, complicated relationships, and years of emotional scars, she’s convinced herself that settling is safer than hoping for something more.

But when an unexpected connection forces her to confront the difference between being loved and being valued, Kimberly begins a journey that challenges everything she thought she deserved. As old wounds resurface and the people closest to her are tested, she must decide whether healing means holding on... or finally finding the strength to let go.

A raw, emotionally charged novel about love, motherhood, resilience, and discovering that sometimes the greatest act of courage is believing you’re worthy of the life you’ve always wanted.

Chapter One · Ain’t Nobody Savin’ Me

The alarm screamed at 6:15 a.m. like it was mad she hadn’t died in her sleep. Kimberly was already half-awake, one foot sticking out from under the covers, the other curled tight into her satin sheets, attempting to hold on to the last bit of warmth. The room was still and dark, nothing but the hum of the ceiling fan slicing through the silence.

Across the hall, Amara’s door hung slightly open. Just enough for Kim to hear the soft rhythm of her daughter’s breathing if her door were open.

This was her favorite part of the day. Before anybody needed anything. Before she had to fake joy for patients at the clinic, rinse hair out of bowls in the kitchen sink, or wear that tired-ass smile her daughter barely looked at anymore. These brief moments of weighted quiet, where she could almost forget she was carrying two lives on her back like luggage with a busted wheel.
